PROJECT 01 / BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Multi-Channel Sales Analytics

A centralized analytics platform for sales, inventory and operational data, designed for scalable reporting and faster management decisions.

SQLBigQueryPower BIDAXPower BI Service
01 / PROBLEM

Fragmented operational data

Sales and operational information lived across multiple business channels and workflows. The goal was to consolidate the data into a centralized reporting system that could support consistent KPIs and decision-making.

02 / APPROACH

Raw → Clean → Gold

A three-layer BigQuery warehouse architecture was used to separate raw ingestion, cleaned business-ready data and reporting-ready models. SQL handled transformation and the resulting model supported scalable BI reporting.
